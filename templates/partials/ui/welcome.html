{% load emmaus %}{% comment %} The welcome of a role (core.onboarding.Welcome; built by core/onboarding.py for the church roles and by events/services/welcome.py for the event roles). It answers, for someone new: where am I (title, role line), what can I do here (the role's screens, one line each), what is missing and what do I do next ("Primeros pasos", each step read from the live data: "Listo" or "Falta", with the screen where it is done). It sits above the next-action panel of the role's home, outside any live region, and is drawn as a bordered white block: the tinted panel of the screen stays the one that asks the next question. "Entendido" puts it away for that role (POST; emmaus.js fades it out and moves the focus on); "Mi cuenta" brings it back.
