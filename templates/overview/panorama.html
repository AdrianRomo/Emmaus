{% extends "base.html" %} {% load community emmaus shell panorama %} {% block page_title %}Panorama{% endblock %} {% block content %} {% comment %} Panorama (overview.views.panorama): how the church is doing, for its pastor and its administration. A dashboard: first what waits for somebody, then the groups, the events, who serves and who is being looked for, each as things (a row, a tile, a bar) and not as sentences. A group is its arch (a gathering), never a person's circle; the follow-ups are their icon. A state is drawn as everywhere else: a request that waits is «Por decidir»'s mark (as on «Grupos de la iglesia»), a follow-up past its day or without an owner is vela (as on «Seguimiento»). PRIVACY. Totals only: the proper names on this page are of groups, events and teams, never of a person, and no link leads to a list of who came. Every number is drawn exactly as overview.services returns it: a number when it is one, its words on a neutral pill when the service gave words, "Menos de N" or "No se muestra" when it kept the total back (the `told` filter, overview/templatetags/panorama.py). Never a blank, never a finer value. A group's last meetings are one mark each: tall when half or more came, short when fewer, the number itself when the service gave it; the words are beside the marks for a screen reader. The rows of a group or an event hold no nested list (facts are

of spans), so a row ends at its own : overview/tests reads one row at a time. {% endcomment %}
