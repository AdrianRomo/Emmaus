A date is its day drawn, the date in words as the way into its page (its data and who signed up are there; a date that passed also offers its opinions) and, on the dates to come, the one action that belongs here: «Este día no hay» / «Sí hay». That answer changes its own row in place (CRAFT §3: it never leads to another step) and the row says what it did: the state word, that the day left Eventos and the public calendar, and that who signed up must be told by the administration. The dates Emmaus did not change and the ones the series left behind are regions of their own (#serie-revisar, #serie-fuera), because «Sí hay» can move a date into them. The one filled action is «Cambiar la serie», in the head. Ending or resuming the series is a step of its own, never in place; ending is quiet text, because the series can be resumed. {% endcomment %} {% now "Y-m-d" as today_iso %}
