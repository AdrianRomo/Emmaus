Most people join Emmaus through an invitation from their church.

Why it exists

People should not be forgotten after they visit, ask questions, or begin following Jesus.

Emmaus gives leaders a shared place to notice questions, assign mentors, follow up personally, and keep discipleship paths clear.

Technology does not replace care here. It helps make care visible enough that people are less likely to slip through the cracks.

For participants

A gentle journey through lessons, reflection, questions, and invitations to talk with trusted leaders.

For mentors

A focused view of assigned people, open questions, and follow-ups that need personal attention.

For pastors and admins

Church-level clarity without turning people into metrics, cases, or tasks.

Safety and privacy matter.

Emmaus handles sensitive spiritual and personal data. Pastoral notes stay hidden from participants, access is role-based, and emergency care belongs with local emergency services.

AI never replaces leaders.

Optional AI drafting is only visible to leaders. A human leader must review, edit, approve, or reject every draft before it becomes a real response.
