One that overlaps is sent as a page, because its answer is the clash page; and if a clash appears between the list and the press, the server answers with that page and the browser shows it. A booking that repeats is one request, decided once (espacios.repeticion): it leads the list, opens its own page and is approved as a page, because the answer is that page (which dates could be booked and which could not). Rejecting has a field: its own page. The two answers are a row's small capsules, the yes first; only the first row's «Aprobar» is filled (the screen's one filled action). {% endcomment %}
