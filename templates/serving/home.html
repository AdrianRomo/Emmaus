Then the teams they are in (who coordinates, by name only) and the days they cannot serve. Nobody else's answer, contact data or unavailable days are here. The role of each date and its order when it has one. A teammate who asks this person to take a date is what needs them now: the one raised surface of the page, first, answered in place (each request is its own region; answered, it leaves and a line keeps what was done; «Tus fechas» and the other requests are read again from the same answer, so the one filled button is always the first thing that still waits: data-inline-also). What this person asked is a list of its own; «Retirar» acts in place. The choice about reminders, when a team of theirs sends them, is one region that says the state and offers the other. Only the two people of a swap and who coordinates the team see it. {% endcomment %}
