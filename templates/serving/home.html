Their dates (the day drawn, what is needed, the team, the time and the place), each its own region: a date that waits is answered in place with the home's two answers; an answered one says the answer and, until the day before, the other answer in place, one tap, exactly as on "Mi iglesia" (one drawing of an answered date); after an answer the focus lands on the answer that is now there (data-inline-focus). Then the teams they are in (who coordinates, by name only) and the days they cannot serve. Nobody else's answer, contact data or unavailable days are here. Wave 10: the role of each date, its order when it has one, a swap asked to or by a teammate (the two people and who coordinates are the only ones who see it), and the choice about reminders when a team of theirs sends them. {% endcomment %}
