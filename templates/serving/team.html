Then the dates that are coming, each with who was asked and what they answered; then who is in the team. Contact data is shown, and can be tapped, because each person is in the team. The other swaps of the team (waiting for the teammate, or done in the last 30 days) are its record, lower. Then the way into roles, rotation, reminders and the order of a date. {% endcomment %}
